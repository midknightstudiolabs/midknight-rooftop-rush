A QUICK STREET SMARTS CHECK

Born to cause a little chaos.

01

Find your lineArrow keys or A / D to switch lanes. Swipe left or right, or tap the LEFT / RIGHT buttons.

02

Move like Midknight↑ / W / Space to jump; hold for height, release for a short hop. ↓ / S to crouch-slide. Swipe up / down on mobile. Gold ramps lead to rooftops.

03

Moon PounceJump again in the air, press Shift / E, or tap POUNCE. Spend one moonlight charge for a higher leap and brief protection from smoke. One pounce per airtime.

04

Wake the cityCatch three floating Moon Gates to light a district, restore a heart and earn 1,000 points. Clean leaps, slides and close dodges chain up to ×8 and refill moonlight. Each charge also recharges in 9 seconds.

05

Choose your skylineChoose routes while running: steer onto a gold ramp to reach a high roof, or stay on the low bridge. Roofs bend gently left and right while the camera stays still. Split skybridges have real gaps—follow the lit roof edges, change lanes early, or jump across.

06

A city that reactsChimneys cough before bursting; amber shutters swing open. Pigeons knock over pots when startled. Gold ramps lead to crumbling shortcuts and laundry lines. Collect gold and land tricks to beat the Night Cat.

Hard objects stop the run with a liquid-cat splat. Tap RUN IT BACK or press Space to restart. Three hearts protect against smoke. Hold N or the NITRO button for Speedster: faster paws, a gold trail and a slow-motion world; release to ease back. Fuel refills from gold and clean tricks. Boost works during jumps too. Fuel drains in real time; solid objects still stop you. blue orbs protect against smoke, pink orbs attract gold. P / Esc pauses. Your best distance stays on this device.
